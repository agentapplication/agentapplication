> **Agent Artifact** is a durable, portable work product with its own identity,
> versions, provenance, permissions, and history.

Agent Applications often produce work that outlives the conversation. A message
belongs to a conversation timeline. An artifact has its own address and
lifecycle. A user can revise it, compare versions, share it outside the original
conversation, or use it as input to later work. Discussing a spreadsheet is
different from having the spreadsheet.

## Portable and live artifacts

A file artifact, such as a document or spreadsheet, can be opened on a user's
computer. A hosted work product, such as an interactive report, website, or
Agent Application, needs a suitable runtime. In both cases, the platform records
the artifact's creator, current version, source lineage, and access rules.

A live artifact may depend on current data that cannot travel with it. Its
record carries governed references and an artifact-scoped grant for the exact
resources it may read. A receiving platform resolves those references and
requests fresh authorization for grants that cannot transfer.
